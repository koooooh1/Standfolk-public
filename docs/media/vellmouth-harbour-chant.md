# Vellmouth Rovers: The Tide-Bell Song

Original chant written for the fictional Brenmoor League demo. The short MP4 uses locally synthesized speech and an original procedural bell, clap, and chord bed; it is not a recording of real supporters.

## Lyrics

Ring the bell, let the harbour know!<br>
Tide and teal, our voices flow!<br>
Vellmouth Rovers, side by side!<br>
Carry us home on the harbour tide!

Vellmouth! Vellmouth! Ring the bell!<br>
Tide and teal, we know you well!<br>
Side by side, through wind and foam!<br>
Vellmouth Rovers, bring us home!

The soundtrack is original and was generated locally from synthesized speech and procedural instruments. It does not use real crowd recordings.
